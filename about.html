Who we are

Rooted in the land.
Here for the farmer.

Vinayramkrishna Agro Tech Pvt. Ltd. is built around a simple belief: healthy soil is the starting point for strong farms and sustainable growth.

We connect farmers with bio-fertilizers, organic manure, crop protection, seeds, soil testing and field guidance. Our aim is to make useful products and dependable support easier to find.
